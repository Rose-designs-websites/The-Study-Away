SQRRR

Survey the work - This method starts with you looking over the topic by scanning the material.

Question - Prompt questions for your self from the topic. Exp:"How can I explain this to a first grader?", "What is this title trying to explain?", "What is the significance of __?"

Read - Properly read the material and be in prusit for answers to the questions you formulated.

Recite - Try and memorize as much as you can and recite to yourself what you know and the main points you learned without looking at the answers.

Review - Read over everything once again to make sure you got all the key points and know the answers to the questions from step 2

Become the teacher

Survey the work and explain the material to yourself.

Without looking at the material, explain out loud what ever you know like you are talking to a 1st grader.

Bridge the gaps in your knowledge by going over the material once again

Finally, with your new knowledge, explain once again the material like your talking to a 1st grader.

Repeat till you feel comfortable with the material..

Blurting method

Start off with writing down everything you know of the material WITHOUT researching,

Go over the topic after writing what you know and look over it to find knowledge gaps,

Repeat process until you are comfortable with the material.

hi


Mock tests

A mock test is a fake/practice test to prepare yourself for the real deal. It also helps you get more used to taking tests and calm your nerves.

Add a 60 minute timer for your mock test

Go online and look for a test on your topic (For example the NYC 2025 grade 8 state test) or simply generate one yourself through AI

This helps you approach tests without your hands shaking and also helps you manage your time on each question.

Change your enviroment

(As a person who reads a lot of self help books) many people who are at least somewhat sucessful say that to build a good habit you NEED to change your enviroment.

This works because as you keep studying in that area, your brain will now proceive that area as a cue to study.

An example of this is you shouldn't study on your bed because you brain thinks of your bed as a cue to rest.

Do not think that you need a lot of space for this either, a good place to study can be your dining table or even a diffrent chair than where you usally sit.

There are diffrent ways to make flash cards, an example is your front will have 3 points of what describes the item, (example;mitochondria) then you will try and answer what it is and check the back to see if it's correct.
