SQRRR

Survey the work - This method starts with you looking over the topic by scanning the material.

Question - Prompt questions for your self from the topic. Exp:"How can I explain this to a first grader?", "What is this title trying to explain?", "What is the significance of __?"

Read - Properly read the material and be in prusit for answers to the questions you formulated.

Recite - Try and memorize as much as you can and recite to yourself what you know and the main points you learned without looking at the answers.

Review - Read over everything once again to make sure you got all the key points and know the answers to the questions from step 2


hi


Become the teacher

Once again, survey the work and explain the material to yourself.

Without looking at the material, explain out loud what ever you know like you are talking to a 1st grader.

Bridge the gaps in your knowledge by going over the material once again

Finally, with your new knowledge, explain once again the material like your talking to a 1st grader.

Repeat till you feel comfortable with the material..

Blurting method

Start off with writing down everything you know of the material WITHOUT researching

Go over the topic after writung what you know and look over it to find knowledge gaps

Repeat process until you are comfortable with the material.
