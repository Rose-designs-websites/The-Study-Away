2 Hour Study session

SQRRR

Survey the work - This method starts with you looking over the topic by scanning the material.

Question - Prompt questions for your self from the topic. Exp:"How can I explain this to a first grader?", "Where is an area i am weak on this subject?", "How can I use __ in my life?"

Read - Properly read the material and be in prusit for answers to the questions you formulated.

Recite - Try and memorize as much as you can and recite to yourself what you know and the main points you learned without looking at the answers.

Review - Read over everything once again to make sure you got all the key points and know the answers to the questions from step 2

Become the teacher

Once again, survey the work and explain the material to yourself.

Without lookimg at the material, explain out loud what ever you know like you are talking to a 1st grader.

Bridge the gaps in your knowladge by going over the material once again

Finally, with your new knowlage, explain once again the material like your talking to a 1st grader.

Repeat till you feel comfortable with the material..
