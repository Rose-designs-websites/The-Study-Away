Canva slide tips

Tip no.1

When making slides that need to have animated transitions, use the transition tool "match & move".

To practice this, place an object on your slide to the left, proceed to duplicate it and move that same object to the right in the duplicated slide. Finally, go in between the slides and set the transition to "match & move"
