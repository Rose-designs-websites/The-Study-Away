Google slides tips

Tip no.1 (Transparent Backgrounds)

For more picturs with clear, transparent background, go into stickers as they will almost always have a transparent background.


Tip no.2 (Color setting)

To have a more appealing slideshows, do not use neon backgrounds for a less tacky look

Try to make sure that your text does not blend in with your background. Instead, you can use more darker or muted colors for text so they still pop out of the background without pulling the viewers attention away from you.

Don't add to many pictures that don't relate to your topic (example: a cute penguin drinking boba on a slide for global warming
